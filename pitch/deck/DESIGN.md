# Deck design rules

Sources: the vault notes `wiki/concepts/ai-assisted-design.md` (de-slopping: remove stuff, no accumulated decoration,
no interchangeable copy or repeated headline formula, real references) and `code-rendered-motion-design.md`
(few words, big; reading order headline → evidence → action).

## Rules
1. One idea per slide. Reading order: headline → one piece of evidence → (optional) one line.
2. Every element must earn its place. No pills, badges, numbered circles, check icons, banners or card grids
   used as decoration. No triads of cards.
3. Evidence is real: app screenshots as captured, numbers with a source. Never redraw app UI.
4. Headlines are plain statements about Ola or the product, written differently on each slide. No "X. Y." slogan
   pairs, no "never/always" taglines stacked on every slide.
5. Say each claim once in the deck (sources/estimates on slide 4 only, the daily scan on slide 6 only).
6. Type: Inter only. Headline 800, 84–140 px; body 34–44 px, 400–500; footnotes 24–26 px. No handwriting, no italics.
7. Colour: warm white `#faf7f0`, ink `#15140f`, muted `#6b665c`, one accent coral `#ff6a3d` used for one thing per
   slide at most, deep green `#12241d` for the first and last slide. No shadows (some PDF viewers turn them into grey boxes).
8. Layout: 130 px side margins, left-aligned, generous empty space; no element touches another.
